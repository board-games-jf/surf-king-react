| SK-DEC-017 | Accepted | Surf King gameplay should **feature frequent dramatic falls without lengthy inactivity**, in a **fast, simple, strategic and replayable race toward the beach**. | SK-DEC-018 | Accepted | **Shark-caused fall resolution, design rule (not implemented):** On a Shark hit reducing a surfer to 0 energy, optionally consume **Lifeguard Float** to remain on that hex at 2 energy or **Swimming Fin** to remain at 1; a rescue does not repeat that Shark entry effect or grant extra movement. Without rescue, the surfer vacates the Shark cell; at the beginning of the next personal turn they return at **2 energy** to a vacant obstacle-free cell directly one legal edge-step toward the sea if possible (otherwise the nearest safe vacant cell no closer to the beach), and may take their normal maneuver on that return turn. Recovery from other fall sources and final obstacle-damage tuning remain open. | Make the cost of falling easy to explain: lose progress unless a scarce rescue card is spent; no skipped turn or repeated hazard loop. | [#11](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/issues/11), [fall and rescue review](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/blob/docs/11-progressive-rules-review/docs/rules-review/FALL_AND_RESCUE.md) |

## Safeguards

- A proposal is never an approved technical decision.
- No component is deemed implemented merely because it appears in the proposed architecture.
- Superseded decisions retain their IDs; record the approved replacement separately.
- The rulebook, MVP requirements and exclusions still need separate validation and approval.
