| SK-DEC-020 | Accepted | **Shark fall and recovery (design only; not implemented):** Entering a Shark inflicts **2 energy damage** (SK-DEC-019). If energy reaches 0, the surfer **falls and stays in that Shark hex**, with no displacement/retreat. At the **start of their next own turn** the surfer stands up there with **1 energy automatically**, or may discard **Swimming Fin to stand at 2 energy** or **Lifeguard Float to stand at 3 energy**. Recovery cards are optional and are used on that next own turn (not as an immediate reaction), without granting extra movement; **whether they consume the turn's normal tactical action slot remains under design review**. The surfer may take their usual legal maneuver on the return turn. Standing up does not re-trigger Shark damage; only a new entry does. Specific attack/forced-displacement interactions while fallen remain open. **SK-DEC-023 now extends the same recovery protocol to all other zero-energy falls.** Supersedes the former sea-ward retreat/immediate-reaction design SK-DEC-018. | Preserve frequent exciting falls and quick return without spatial setback; differentiate scarce rescue cards while allowing no-card recovery. | [#11](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/issues/11), [draft PR #12](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/pull/12), [fall and rescue review](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/blob/docs/11-progressive-rules-review/docs/rules-review/FALL_AND_RESCUE.md) |

| SK-DEC-021 | Accepted | **Occupancy of a fallen surfer on a Shark hex (design only; not implemented):** A surfer fallen at 0 energy continues to **occupy their Shark hex** until they recover and leave. Other surfers **cannot enter that occupied hex by a normal maneuver**, cannot overlap the fallen surfer, and **cannot perform a normal Drop In against the fallen surfer** while they are down. Another surfer must choose a different legal move, or any other independently permitted action; the fallen surfer still uses the SK-DEC-020 next-own-turn 1/2/3 energy recovery and may maneuver normally. This decision does **not** approve forced displacement, card-targeting exceptions, or an automatic all-neighbors-blocked fallback; review those separately. | Preserve a simple one-surfer-per-cell board invariant, avoid forcibly moving or attacking a surfer who has already fallen, and allow the short-lived occupied tile to have tactical significance without inventing a retreat penalty. | [#11](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/issues/11), [draft PR #12](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/pull/12), [fall and rescue review](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/blob/docs/11-progressive-rules-review/docs/rules-review/FALL_AND_RESCUE.md) |

| SK-DEC-022 | Accepted | **Mandatory normal movement when possible (design only; not implemented):** On an eligible player's normal maneuver step, **they must move to one legally accessible edge-adjacent hex if at least one exists**. They may choose any legal direction, including sideways or backward, but **may not voluntarily pass, stand still, or use Skip/Rest instead of that maneuver**. If **no legal normal maneuver exists**, the movement step may finish without displacement solely as a **forced blocked-state exception**. An optional card or normal Drop In does not by itself waive the required maneuver; any special action that counts as/displaces the maneuver requires a separate approved rule. On the recovery turn after a Shark fall (SK-DEC-020), normal movement is also required when legal. The precise automatic blocked-turn transition, card eligibility and whether any blocked-turn bonus applies remain under review. | Keep the race progressing and distinguish genuine board congestion from voluntary inactivity without reintroducing a Rest action or an all-pass stalemate. Explicit owner clarification on 2026-11-17; the current UI and engine still expose `Skip` / `pass` in the maneuver stage, so code alignment will require a separately authorized implementation. | [#11](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/issues/11), [draft PR #12](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/pull/12), [turn flow review](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/blob/docs/11-progressive-rules-review/docs/rules-review/TURNS_AND_PACING.md) |

| SK-DEC-023 | Accepted | **Unified recovery after any fall (design only; not implemented):** When a surfer reaches **0 energy** due to a Shark, another terrain effect, a card such as Big Wave/Sunburn, or a Drop In/combat result, they **fall and remain on the hex where the triggering effect finishes**, without a retreat/teleport solely because they fell. That hex remains occupied by the fallen surfer (one surfer per hex; no normal move can overlap them). They stand up **at the start of their next personal turn**, on that **same hex**, with **1 energy automatically**, or optionally discard **Swimming Fin for 2 energy total** or **Lifeguard Float for 3 energy total**. Recovery cards are chosen on that personal turn, never as immediate reactions. After standing, the surfer may make their **ordinary legal maneuver in that same turn** and **must move when one is available** (SK-DEC-022); recovery does not grant bonus movement or a fully skipped return turn. Standing up is not a new obstacle entry. This generalizes the Shark-specific protocol SK-DEC-020 without superseding Shark −2 (SK-DEC-019) or fallen-Shark Drop In exclusion (SK-DEC-021). **Still open:** The tactical action-slot cost of a recovery card, card/forced displacement or swap during a fall, normal Drop In eligibility against fallen surfers outside the Shark-specific SK-DEC-021 scope, full blockage handling and other card/attack numerical values. | Apply one easy-to-teach quick-rejoin rule to all falls, preserving progress and participation without inventing different respawns for cards and combat. The owner explicitly approved generalizing the prior Shark 1/2/3 model in the gameplay review. | [#11](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/issues/11), [draft PR #12](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/pull/12), [fall and rescue review](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/blob/docs/11-progressive-rules-review/docs/rules-review/FALL_AND_RESCUE.md) |

## Safeguards

- A proposal is never an approved technical decision.
- No component is deemed implemented merely because it appears in the proposed architecture.
- Superseded decisions retain their IDs; record the approved replacement separately.
- The rulebook, MVP requirements and exclusions still need separate validation and approval.
