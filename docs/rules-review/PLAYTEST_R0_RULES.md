# Surf King — R0 tabletop rules (experimental, NOT approved as the final game)

**Date:** 2026-11-23. **Purpose:** Give the owner one **internally consistent, playable candidate** for manual 2-, 4- and 7-person sessions while the assistant runs model simulations. This is a **facilitation fixture**, not a rules ratification, application change, online multiplayer authorization, or claim of a completed human playtest. Issue [#11](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/issues/11), draft PR [#12](https://github.com/board-games-jf/surf-king-react/pull/12). Source geometry is the official 53-cell `src/map/layout/board-layout.json` at main `c6fd08e2da82ca223740e3aa944c47f87aa84f16`.

## 0. What is approved and what is only trial material?

**Approved in the draft gameplay review:** 2 starting energy; mandatory legal normal edge-neighbor movement; Shark entry −2; no extra Shark damage from merely standing there; a surfer fallen at 0 **stays on the occupied tile**, gets up at start of their next own turn with 1 energy free, 2 with Swimming Fin or 3 with Lifeguard Float; spending Fin/Float takes the normal tactic slot; one randomly chosen eligible **hand** card is lost per new fall (transferred to responsible opponent for a player-caused fall, otherwise discarded). Meaningful interaction between human players is an approved design goal.

**Only for R0 evaluation, NOT finally approved:** the 60-card B60 composition, every changed card effect and range, Surf Current, maximum energy 5, 2 opening hand cards, 5-card hand limit, 1 tactical action per turn, 0-energy normal movement, dice configuration and damage for Drop In, a preparation-only first turn, one draw after moving, obstacle lifetime/caps, attribution conventions, hand-overflow policy, Drop In optional standing swap and finish/tie observation rule. Main `SurfKingGame.js` still follows its old rules. **Do not copy this document verbatim into production code without a later owner-approved decision.**

## 1. Setup (2, 4 or 7 participants)

- Use the existing **53 numbered connected hexes**, fixed Sharks at **28, 29, 30 and 31**, and beach cells **49, 50, 51 and 52**. An edge-adjacent move must follow a genuine shared edge, **not** a diagonally touching point or numeric cell-ID offset.
- Player seats start at cells **0, 1, ... n−1** in seat order. Give each surfer **2 energy** and deal **2 cards from the shuffled B60 deck** (see [R0 card reference](PLAYTEST_R0_CARDS.md)). Set aside a face-up discard pile and markers for placed obstacles and active defenses. Physical copies remain distinct.
- Each player may hold **at most 5 unplayed cards**, and has **maximum 5 energy** in the pilot. Record who acts first and **rotate first actor in the next comparable match**, holding starting cells/deck opening order as similar as possible.
- Only one surfboard/character piece can occupy any cell, including a fallen surfer's cell. A placed Stone blocks entry; other placed obstacles allow entry and apply their effects. Sharks cannot be picked up or removed by Jumping in R0.

## 2. Each personal turn (very short sequence)

1. **Start / recovery:** Remove this player's expiring active protections and obstacles. If fallen, stand **on the same cell** at **E1 free**, or choose to spend **one Fin for E2** or **one Float for E3** (a used Fin/Float consumes the tactic for this turn). Standing on Shark does not cause another −2. There is no waiting turn.
2. **One optional tactic:** Use **one valid card OR attempt one adjacent Drop In OR neither**. A card must have a legal target/destination; if none, keep it. A Drop In is edge-adjacent only and targets a **standing** rival. A fall during one's own tactic ends the turn without a maneuver. **First own turn:** preparation, healing, protection and obstacle placement are allowed; **direct damage and opponent displacement** (Big Wave, Sunburn, Change, Tsunami, Drop In) are not. This opening restriction is **trial-only**.
3. **Required maneuver:** If the surfer is standing and can reach **any vacant non-Stone edge-neighbor**, they **must move one edge**, even if that is sideways, backwards or into danger. Normal movement itself costs **0 energy in R0**. If **every neighbor** is occupied or blocked, stay in place, note "forced blocked"; voluntary passing is prohibited. A successful standing Drop In **exchange**, Change, or Surf Current may replace/extend this maneuver as specified below: never claim an extra ordinary move for an exchange.
4. **Resolve arrival:** New Shark entry **−2**; Storm entry **−1**; Island grants **+2 once** (remove it); Cyclone redirects **once at most** to a randomly selected legal neighboring cell, excluding the cell just left, resolving any new arrival terrain. If no valid Cyclone exit, remain on the Cyclone cell. Do not recursively redirect twice in one maneuver. If a fall occurs, remain at 0 energy on that last cell and resolve the single random-card penalty.
5. **Draw:** If the surfer finished their action with a successful maneuver **and is upright**, draw **one** card only if their unplayed hand currently has **fewer than five cards**. A swap counts as one maneuver; two-step Surf Current earns **one draw**, not two. No draw on a forced blocked turn, while fallen, or if already holding five. When the deck empties, shuffle the discard pile into a new draw deck, leaving active/board cards where they are.

## 3. Drop In and falling

- **Normal Drop In:** attacker and defender must stand on **edge-adjacent** cells, defender not protected against Drop In. Both roll **one d6 at E1** or **two d6 at E2–E5**; compare **the highest die on each side** (never add dice). On a tie, no damage or exchange. On a strict result, **loser takes −1 energy**. Damage happens before considering position.
- **Trial-only optional exchange:** If the **attacker wins**, **both surfers remain upright**, and their cells are still eligible, the attacker **may exchange positions** with the defender. The exchange **replaces the attacker's mandatory maneuver**; both resolve their newly entered terrain, and the attacker gets at most one end-of-turn draw. If either falls from the combat outcome, **no exchange**; a fallen defender retains their occupied hex. If the attacker wins without exchanging and remains upright, the usual legal maneuver still follows.
- **General falling:** On a **genuine new fall to 0 energy**, choose **one uniformly random eligible unplayed card** from that person's hand, if any. If another player directly knocked them down (damaging offensive card, Drop In, or card-forced hazardous displacement in the R0 fixture), the **responsible player receives the card**. If terrain/impersonal hazard caused the fall, put it in discard. No eligible hand card means no loss. **Never steal and discard twice for one fall**, and never take an already active/deployed card. Further effects while down do not cause a second loss.
- **Pilot attribution conventions, not accepted rules:** If the **initiator** falls by losing their Drop In, **the defender** receives the randomly lost card; if they fell because they intentionally moved themselves into Shark using Change, treat this as an **unassisted hazard** and discard. If an opponent's **direct forced displacement** pushed the surfer into Shark, credit that opponent; if a Stone merely caused them to choose a different legal move into Shark, treat as terrain and discard.
- **Full recipient hand:** First transfer the stolen card, then if the recipient has **six**, they **choose any one unplayed hand card to discard**, including the newly won card. No extra card loss is charged to the person who fell. This is a temporary overflow fixture only.

## 4. Victory in R0

The **first surfer to enter any beach cell (49–52) is the provisional winner**. For *observation only*, let the remaining players complete **only their mandatory movement** in the **same round** (no more tactics, attacks or draws), and record how many also reach the beach; this is **not** a formally approved round-end victory rule or fair tie-breaker. Finished surfers freeze in their beach cells. Do not forcibly overlap or evict them. Report a match with no beach arrival by **round 16** as **unfinished**, without inventing a winner.

This observation tail allows the owner to detect the known first-actor/parallel-lane problem. An alternative finish/tie rule should be tested separately after seeing those observations.

## 5. What the owner should record during manual games

Keep it simple: match ID, 2/4/7 participants, who started, whether each player had meaningful opportunity to act against someone, round and **actual minutes** until provisional finish, any special or unclear card resolution, number of attacks/blocked turns/falls/steals, whether someone felt targeted unfairly, and **would-be same-round co-finishers**. Afterward each person answers the five brief questions in [R0 scorecard](PLAYTEST_R0_SCORECARD.md) or [human protocol](HUMAN_PLAYTEST_PROTOCOL.md). **Only human observations can validate enjoyment and social interaction.**

## 6. Boundary and governance notes

- This kit deliberately fixes ambiguous pilot mechanics so sessions are repeatable. It **does not** mark those mechanics Accepted or replace the existing `main` implementation.
- Original gameplay decision IDs SK-DEC-019–024 on draft PR #12 **conflict** with different accepted visual IDs in `main`. Renumber/reconcile before any future integration; never overwrite accepted visual decisions.
- When a situation is unclear, note the event and the interpretation used instead of silently inventing a new permanent rule. Compare one changed pilot rule at a time.
- **This is a human-test kit, not a claim that the assistant has conducted human matches.** The owner performs those sessions; the assistant can continue simulations and analyze logs they provide. No gameplay code, merge, deployment or paid service has been authorized.
