## Suggested minimal manual series

1. **Two players:** Play two R0 games, switching who starts while keeping starting cells/deck order comparable. Focus on attacks/counterplay/initiative. Capture an opening where Big Wave or Stone is available, if possible.
2. **Four players:** One exploratory R0 game; note disputes over obstacles, forced hazards, positional swap and whether 7-ish rounds feels short enough.
3. **Seven players:** One exploratory R0 game; time full play and waiting, check repeated targeting and whether every person has a meaningful decision at least once.
4. If a specific mechanic is troublesome, repeat **only one changed rule** in a comparable match; do not edit the deck, win condition and attack range simultaneously.

These counts are a **starting logistics suggestion**, not enough to establish statistical fairness. There is **no claim that the owner has performed any of these games yet**.
